INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT: COMPLIANCE COORDINATOR

The trigger is usually an email from a subcontractor or broker. I open the
attachment, compare the legal name and holder against our spreadsheet, check
the general liability limit and expiration, then route anything unusual to the
project manager. The odd cases are a DBA name, a missing waiver, an expired
policy on arrival, a duplicate certificate, and a scan where the endorsement
cannot be read. I write questions for the sponsor when the evidence is not
enough and keep the old certificate because an auditor may ask what we knew on
a particular day.
