STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SUBCONTRACTOR CERTIFICATE RENEWAL INTAKE

When a certificate arrives in the shared compliance inbox, the coordinator
downloads the PDF, matches the named insured to the vendor record, checks the
certificate holder and coverage limits, and records the expiry date. If the
additional-insured endorsement is missing, the coordinator creates an
exception for the project owner. If the scan is unreadable or two policies are
attached, the coordinator asks the broker a question before a sponsor decides
whether the work may continue. The completed verification and the exception
decision are stored with the vendor record.
