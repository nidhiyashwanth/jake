# Discovery interview form

Use this form with the person who performs the workflow. Record with consent,
use the customer's terminology, and attach only customer-approved artifacts.
The output is a reviewable discovery draft, exception taxonomy, signed baseline,
and opportunity score—not an unverified requirements transcript.

## Session record

- Customer / workspace: `[name]`
- Workflow owner and sponsor: `[names and roles]`
- Operator(s) interviewed: `[names and roles]`
- Date / consent reference: `[date and reference]`
- Systems and inboxes in scope: `[sources]`
- Document types in scope: `[explicit v1 list]`

## Shape and volume

1. What starts the work, and where does it arrive?
2. Walk through one item from arrival to done while sharing the real screens.
3. What is the daily/monthly volume and spike pattern?
4. What are p50 and p90 handling times? How were they observed?
5. Which people, spreadsheets, inboxes, and systems are touched?

## Judgement and exceptions

1. What is checked first, and why?
2. When is a document not trusted even when it looks complete?
3. What are the five common failure paths and their frequencies?
4. Which rare or expensive failures have happened in the last year?
5. Who is consulted when the operator is unsure?
6. Which exceptions stay human by design?

## Baseline and value

- Active vendors / records: `[measured count]`
- Verifications per month: `[measured count]`
- Minutes per instance p50 / p90: `[observed values]`
- People touching the workflow: `[count/FTE equivalent]`
- Loaded cost per hour: `[value and source]`
- Error and rework rates: `[sample sizes and values]`
- Cycle time from receipt to verified: `[value]`
- Lapse incidents and cost: `[value or explicitly unknown]`
- Annual audit-preparation hours: `[value or explicitly unknown]`
- Estimated inputs and disputes: `[list]`

## Politics and adoption

1. Whose number improves if this works?
2. Who will be nervous about the change?
3. What would operators do with time returned by the workflow?
4. Who can stop the pilot, and who can sign acceptance?
5. What customer obligations could delay the work?

## Outputs and approval

- [ ] Discovery draft reviewed by the workflow owner.
- [ ] Exception taxonomy mapped to rule, prompt/schema change, or accepted
      human-review driver.
- [ ] Baseline versioned and signed by the sponsor.
- [ ] Golden-set source rights and labels recorded.
- [ ] Opportunity score is server-bound to the signed baseline.
- [ ] Scope, out-of-scope items, and change-control owner are explicit.
